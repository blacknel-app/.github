# Blacknel

The central nervous system for digital brand operations.

We unify inbox, social publishing, reviews & reputation, ads management, and analytics into a single workspace — with AI that's trained on every brand's voice.

## What we do

- 🌐 **Unified inbox** across Facebook Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Google Business
- 📤 **Multi-channel publishing** to 10 networks with brand voice consistency checks
- ⭐ **Reviews & reputation** with crisis detection
- 🤖 **AI operations** anchored to your brand voice docs
- 📊 **Cross-channel analytics** with industry benchmarks
- 📢 **Unified ads management** for Google, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok

## Built for

Marketing agencies, multi-location chains, and DTC brands operating across LATAM and the US.
